# ThinkTrail

> Trace your thinking across AI conversations. Turn scattered Cowork sessions into an interactive, editable mindmap.

ThinkTrail scans your Claude Cowork session history, extracts topic hierarchies, merges related discussions across sessions, and generates a draggable, editable HTML mindmap — so you never lose track of where your ideas started and where they branched.

## Quick Start

```
/thinktrail
```

Pick sessions. Confirm the outline. Open the mindmap.

## Why

Multiple AI chat windows, multiple threads, multiple days. You forget what you discussed, where you went deep, and what you barely touched. ThinkTrail rebuilds your thinking trail from your Cowork transcripts.

## Features

- **Incremental by default** — only reads new sessions after the first run
- **Cross-session merging** — same topic discussed in different sessions? Merged under one node
- **Editable mindmap** — double-click to edit, drag to reorganize, right-click to add/remove
- **Full refresh mode** — `--full --sessions <ids>` when you want a fresh extraction
